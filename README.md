# ECommerce Customer Churn - SQL Capstone Project
## Introduction
The E‑Commerce Customer Churn – SQL Capstone Project focuses on analyzing customer transaction data to identify churn patterns and retention opportunities using SQL. Raw datasets were cleaned and transformed with SQL queries, handling duplicates, missing values, and inconsistent records to ensure accuracy. Advanced SQL techniques such as joins, aggregations, subqueries, and window functions were applied to engineer features like purchase frequency, recency, and loyalty scores. The final reports highlight churn rates, risk segments, and customer behavior trends, providing actionable insights for improving retention strategies and supporting data‑driven business decisions.

## Data Preparation in SQL
### Data Cleaning  
Removed duplicates, standardized customer IDs, and handled missing transaction records to ensure accuracy.

### Data Transformation  
Applied SQL queries to normalize purchase dates, unify product categories, and correct inconsistent entries.

### Feature Engineering  
Created metrics such as purchase frequency, recency (days since last purchase), and loyalty scores using aggregations and window functions.

### Advanced SQL Techniques  
Leveraged joins, subqueries, and ranking functions to segment customers into churn risk categories.

## Analytical Reporting
### Churn Rate Analysis  
Calculated overall churn percentage and tracked changes over time.

### Risk Segmentation  
Classified customers into high, medium, and low churn risk groups based on recency and frequency scores.

### Behavioral Trends  
Identified patterns such as declining purchase frequency or reduced basket size before churn.

### Revenue Impact  
Quantified lost revenue from churned customers and highlighted high-value segments at risk.

## Key Insights
### High-Risk Customers  
Customers with low purchase frequency and long inactivity periods are most likely to churn.

### Loyalty Drivers  
Frequent buyers with consistent purchase patterns show strong retention, especially when engaged with loyalty programs.

### Product Influence  
Customers who primarily purchase discounted items churn faster than those buying premium products.

### Seasonality  
Churn spikes after festive seasons, suggesting one-time buyers who don’t return post-discounts.

## Recommendations
### Retention Campaigns  
Target high-risk customers with personalized offers, reminders, and loyalty rewards.

### Customer Segmentation  
Use SQL-driven risk scores to tailor engagement strategies for different segments.

### Product Strategy  
Encourage premium product adoption through bundling and exclusive deals to reduce churn.

### Post-Festive Engagement  
Implement follow-up campaigns after festive seasons to convert one-time buyers into repeat customers.

### Predictive Modeling  
Extend SQL outputs into machine learning models to forecast churn and automate interventions.

## Strategic Impact
This project demonstrates how SQL-based feature engineering and reporting can transform raw transaction data into actionable churn insights. By integrating churn metrics into dashboards, businesses can proactively identify at-risk customers, optimize retention strategies, and safeguard long-term revenue growth.
